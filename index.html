Built From a Real Film · HOW I SEE (2025, 15 min, B&W)

Made in San Francisco’s Tenderloin. Proven on real street interviews.

Shot in stark black and white on the streets of San Francisco’s Tenderloin, HOW I SEE follows Ukrainian filmmaker Zhan Petrov as he confronts the visual and human parallels between an American neighborhood left behind and the war tearing through his homeland.

On the first night of shooting, Petrov and his crew were attacked on the street and their equipment was damaged—yet technicians salvaged every frame from the damaged phones. He returned with a local guide and finished the film at eye level.

Hours of street interviews. Every word had to stay true to the person who said it. That’s why Script Lab exists.

Strict Editorial Provenance

Never confuse real human speech with AI or rewritten lines.

Born inside 220 Lab’s cinéma-vérité production rooms, Film Script Lab enforces four visually unmistakable dialogue modes on the page and in every exported file.

ORIGINAL · Verbatim

Locked Spoken Truth

Pulled directly from the YouTube video with a truncated SHA-1 SRC- source ID and timestamp. Strictly read-only so nobody can accidentally alter a subject’s actual words.

Solid Forest Green · Clickable Timecode
ADAPTED · Linked

Shaped for Cinema, Anchored to Source

Trimmed or tightened for rhythm while keeping the verbatim spoken quote and YouTube timestamp permanently attached underneath—with one-click Restore Original.

Dashed Amber · Verbatim Drawer Attached
AI SUGGESTION

Transparent Dramatic Bridges

Need a structural transition between two real interview moments? Synthetic suggestions are quarantined in dotted purple and can never masquerade as ORIGINAL speech.

Dotted Purple · Zero False Attribution
WRITTEN · Authored

Pure Screenwriter Invention

Dialogue typed directly by you or your co-writer with no video source. Cleanly marked in double-ruled slate during editorial review and formatted seamlessly on PDF print.

Double Slate · Classic Screenplay Cue

Questions & Answers

Everything filmmakers ask before switching.

Does Film Script Lab really work with any YouTube video?

Yes. Paste any public or unlisted YouTube URL (interviews, rehearsals, archival broadcasts, Shorts, or livestreams). Our edge ingestion API extracts captions, timestamps, and speaker turns automatically. For raw camera uploads that don’t have captions on YouTube yet, you can drop in any .srt, .vtt, or Whisper JSON file—or run our included Apple Silicon mlx-whisper script—and every line still links frame-accurately to your YouTube player.

Why is software access gated by an approval waitlist?

220 Lab builds precision filmmaking instruments with working directors and editors. Reviewing waitlist cohorts lets us onboard productions cleanly, protect edge caption ingestion capacity, and support every filmmaker personally—while offering Founding Pass checkout for productions that need immediate day-one access.

How does the Source Provenance CSV support documentary E&O review?

Errors & Omissions (E&O) review often requires showing where verbatim quotes came from and separating exact speech from dramatized adaptation. Every ORIGINAL and ADAPTED line in Film Script Lab carries a truncated SHA-1 SRC- source ID, original spoken text, final screenplay text, and a clickable timestamp link in a source-linked provenance ledger to support E&O review.

Can I move my screenplay into Final Draft, Highland, or Fade In?

Instantly. Press ⌘E to export standard .fountain syntax (compatible with Final Draft, Highland 2, Fade In, WriterDuet, and Arc Studio) with provenance notes preserved in non-printing [[brackets]], or print industry-standard 12pt Courier Prime US Letter PDFs directly from your browser.
