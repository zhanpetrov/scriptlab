Questions & Answers

Everything filmmakers ask before switching.

Does Film Script Lab really work with any YouTube video?

Yes. Paste any public or unlisted YouTube URL (interviews, rehearsals, archival broadcasts, Shorts, or livestreams). Our edge ingestion API extracts captions, timestamps, and speaker turns automatically. For raw camera uploads that don’t have captions on YouTube yet, you can drop in any .srt, .vtt, or Whisper JSON file—or run our included Apple Silicon mlx-whisper script—and every line still links frame-accurately to your YouTube player.

Why is software access gated by an approval waitlist?

220 Lab builds precision filmmaking instruments with working directors and editors. Reviewing waitlist cohorts lets us onboard productions cleanly, protect edge caption ingestion capacity, and support every filmmaker personally—while offering Founding Pass checkout for productions that need immediate day-one access.

How does the Source Provenance CSV help with documentary E&O insurance?

Errors & Omissions (E&O) clearance requires proving where verbatim quotes came from and distinguishing exact speech from dramatized adaptation. Every ORIGINAL and ADAPTED line in Film Script Lab carries a SHA-1 SRC- ID, original spoken text, final screenplay text, and a clickable https://youtu.be/...?t=124s timestamp link in one clean CSV ledger.

Can I move my screenplay into Final Draft, Highland, or Fade In?

Instantly. Press ⌘E to export standard .fountain syntax (compatible with Final Draft, Highland 2, Fade In, WriterDuet, and Arc Studio) with provenance notes preserved in non-printing [[brackets]], or print industry-standard 12pt Courier Prime US Letter PDFs directly from your browser.
